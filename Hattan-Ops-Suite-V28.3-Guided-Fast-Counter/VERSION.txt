Hattan Ops Suite V28.3 — Guided Fast Counter

Changes from V28.2:
- Tag Assign now includes ONLY Dry Cleaning and Alterations.
- Laundered Shirts are excluded from physical tag assignment.
- Wash & Fold remains excluded from physical tag assignment.
- Customer Lookup is larger and easier to use at the counter.
- Service tabs and garment buttons use larger touch targets and type.
- Add Services now follows: 1. Garment Type, 2. Quantity, 3. Details, 4. Color.
- Dry Cleaning has a 1–9 quantity keypad plus a direct typed quantity field with Enter support.
- The old All Garments dropdown is replaced by a large More Garment Types pop-up.
- Garment color and Wash & Fold bag color are never assumed; staff must select them.
- Laundered Shirts has a much larger quantity field; Enter adds the shirts.
- Customer profiles now have optional automatic shirt packaging, starch and pants-crease defaults with one-time override confirmation.
- This Customer Visit stays visible and scrollable while moving down the counter page.
- Up/Down arrow keys scroll the Counter page when no input is active.
- Color choices use larger visible color swatches.
- Simple Counter receives the same quantity keypad and color-to-add behavior.
