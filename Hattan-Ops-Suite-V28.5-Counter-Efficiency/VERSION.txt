Hattan Ops Suite V28.5 — Counter Efficiency

Changes from corrected V28.4:
- AI intake recognizes and distributes colors for Laundered Shirts, Shirt on Hanger and Wash & Press shirts.
- Multiple stated shirt colors become separate lines while the spoken total remains the exact piece count.
- Laundered Shirts also have visible manual color choices.
- Alterations now show garment color choices and preserve the selected color on the alteration and linked dry-clean line.
- This Customer Visit has no independent scrollbar; it follows the main POS page scroll.
- Counter spacing is denser and uses more horizontal layout without reducing the readable type sizes.
- Line price/no-charge/note controls collapse behind a compact edit row to keep the visit summary short.
- Customer lookup uses relevance scoring instead of an anywhere-in-the-record substring filter.
- One- and two-letter name searches only return customers whose first or last name starts with those letters.
- Multi-word names match in either first-name/last-name order; phone, customer number, address, apartment, ticket, barcode and tag remain searchable.

Carried forward from corrected V28.4:
- The final receipt is now generated directly by V28.4 instead of relying on an older-ticket HTML replacement.
- Delivery apartment/unit is rendered as a large, black, bold, centered ticket hero.
- Receipt content uses a conservative 68 mm print width with protected right-side money columns.
- This Customer Visit remains sticky inside the actual scrolling POS surface, including narrower counter layouts.
- Up/Down arrows scroll the active POS page or modal whenever the employee is not typing in a field.
- Multi-quantity dry cleaning is entered one piece per color tap; garment and special material stay selected while the remaining count decreases.
- Repeated taps on the same color merge correctly, while different colors remain separate lines.
- AI intake distributes explicit colors across the stated quantity without multiplying the piece count.
- “3 blouses, one is blue, navy, green silk” is verified as exactly 3 pieces, one per color, all with silk upcharge.
- AI/voice source wording is removed from the customer’s printed ticket.

Changes from V28.3:
- Garment and Wash & Fold bag colors now include explicit None and Multi Color choices.
- Dry-cleaning prices can be retyped for one-off scenarios, both before and after adding a line.
- A large No Charge / Do Over control is available before and after adding a garment.
- Stain, Delicate and Defective Condition flags can be added to each garment.
- Each garment/order line has its own editable notes field.
- Printed apartment/unit numbers are large, bold and centered near the top of every ticket.
- Thermal-ticket totals have extra right-side clearance so subtotal and balance amounts do not clip.
- Imported multi-piece garment descriptions print as individually spaced lines.
- Printed piece totals are recalculated from line quantities, including legacy imported tickets.

Carried forward from V28.3:
- Tag Assign now includes ONLY Dry Cleaning and Alterations.
- Laundered Shirts are excluded from physical tag assignment.
- Wash & Fold remains excluded from physical tag assignment.
- Customer Lookup is larger and easier to use at the counter.
- Service tabs and garment buttons use larger touch targets and type.
- Add Services now follows: 1. Garment Type, 2. Quantity, 3. Details, 4. Color.
- Dry Cleaning has a 1–9 quantity keypad plus a direct typed quantity field with Enter support.
- The old All Garments dropdown is replaced by a large More Garment Types pop-up.
- Garment color and Wash & Fold bag color are never assumed; staff must select them.
- Laundered Shirts has a much larger quantity field; Enter adds the shirts.
- Customer profiles now have optional automatic shirt packaging, starch and pants-crease defaults with one-time override confirmation.
- This Customer Visit stays visible and scrollable while moving down the counter page.
- Up/Down arrow keys scroll the Counter page when no input is active.
- Color choices use larger visible color swatches.
- Simple Counter receives the same quantity keypad and color-to-add behavior.
