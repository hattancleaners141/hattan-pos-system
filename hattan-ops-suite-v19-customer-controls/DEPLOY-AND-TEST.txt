V19 ACCEPTANCE TESTS
0. Customer finance at New Ticket
   - Select a customer with an unpaid balance and store credit.
   - Confirm both amounts appear at the upper right.
   - Open Generate Report, Text Report and Email Report.
   - Choose Pay Now and verify store credit remains off until staff chooses Yes.
   - Confirm the 3% card fee is calculated only on the amount left after credit.

1. Delivery-default safeguard
   - Set a customer profile to Delivery and start a new ticket.
   - Confirm Delivery is selected automatically.
   - Choose Pickup and confirm the warning appears.
   - Keep Delivery once, then test the one-ticket Pickup override.
   - Confirm the saved customer profile still says Delivery afterward.

2. Tag Assign and bilingual production
   - Confirm Dry Cleaning, Laundered Shirts and Alterations appear in Tag Assign.
   - Confirm Wash & Fold never appears and cannot be added by barcode scan.
   - Confirm White is available and Black is absent.
   - In Wash & Fold, select Low dry, No softener, Delicate cycle and
     Separate darks & whites; confirm bold Chinese appears under each English label.

3. Thermal ticket priorities
   - Print a Delivery customer with apartment 2C and confirm `2C *` is very large at top.
   - Print a same-day order and confirm RUSH is very large at top.
   - Confirm the receipt cuts immediately after the barcode with no long blank feed.

V18 STABILITY REGRESSION TESTS
0. Login layout
   - Employee names and Manager badges stay inside their cards.
   - Change PIN is a separate full-width row and does not cover the name.
   - Tab and Enter operate Sign In and Change PIN as separate controls.

1. Secure setup
   - Settings > Live System Setup shows Netlify, Supabase and Clover status.
   - The private Clover token must only be entered in Netlify Environment
     variables. There is intentionally no private-token input in the POS.

2. Shared counters
   - Open two browsers and sign in.
   - Create/edit a customer or ticket in one.
   - Confirm the other updates within five seconds without a refresh.
   - Optional ES256 Realtime variables reduce that to an instant push update.
   - Disconnect one browser, make a change, reconnect, and confirm the
     optimistic conflict merge preserves both counters' records.

3. Secure staff accounts
   - Complete the one-time manager bootstrap.
   - Add a staff member and change a PIN.
   - Confirm five incorrect attempts cause a 15-minute rate limit.
   - Punch in from a separate device without taking over the counter user.

4. Clover sandbox card-on-file
   - Add a card from a customer profile using Clover-hosted fields.
   - Confirm only brand/last four appear after save.
   - Replace and remove a saved card; confirm the old Clover source is revoked.
   - Run one sandbox batch charge and one manager-authorized refund.
   - Confirm the Clover charge/refund IDs stay attached to ticket history.

5. Existing workflows
   - Separate service tickets and due dates, RUSH, AI Voice Intake.
   - Post-intake tag assignment, rack/conveyor scan, recalls.
   - Delivery batch scan, timestamp, print and proof photo.
   - Pickup, pickup recall, full ticket editing, store credit, A/R search.
   - Star TSP100IV 80mm printing and NADAMOO Code 128 scan + Enter.

6. Star receipt length
   - In Chrome choose Star TSP100, 72mm x Receipt, Portrait, scale 100%,
     margins None, headers/footers Off, and background graphics On.
   - Print one hardware test ticket first.
   - Confirm it cuts directly after the barcode/test footer without feeding a
     long unused section of the preprinted roll.

7. V18 ticket and production checks
   - Wash & Fold prints bag color and detergent/dry preferences in larger bold text.
   - RUSH prints in a bold box at the very top and defaults to 4:00 PM.
   - Apartment/unit prints in large type at the top of the ticket.
   - Change the default RUSH time in Settings and override it on one ticket.
   - Confirm special-material upcharges are added once per line, not per piece.
   - Dry Cleaning, Laundered Shirts and Alterations appear in Tag Assign;
     Wash & Fold does not. White is available and Black is removed.

8. Windows voice and startup checks
   - Add OPENAI_API_KEY as a secret Netlify environment variable and redeploy.
   - Settings > Windows Voice Intake > Test Windows Microphone shows a moving meter.
   - Choose the correct Windows input; this choice stays on that counter.
   - Use the main microphone button to record, stop and transcribe, then review
     the transcript before Interpret.
   - Dictate multiple services and confirm each becomes a separate ticket draft.
   - Reload while signed in; the secure session restores without sample staff or
     the wrong page flashing. A normal reload does not require another PIN.
   - Set one due date to today and confirm RUSH appears onscreen and at the top
     of the printed ticket automatically.
   - Confirm the full Tomorrow button is visible on the Windows counter.
   - Tab through Back to Counter and buttons; no typing caret should appear on them.

9. Legacy import pilot
   - Manager downloads a backup before importing.
   - Import a small customer CSV first and verify address, apartment, balance,
     store credit, customer number, notes and delivery/pickup default.
   - Remove one incorrect preview row and confirm it is not imported.
   - Import a small ticket-history file and verify partial balances and dates.
   - Screenshot/photo extraction is optional and requires OPENAI_API_KEY in Netlify.
     Review every extracted row; never upload payment-card images or passwords.

10. Secure new-customer card flow
   - In shared mode, New Customer does not accept raw card data.
   - Create the customer, open the customer profile, and use Add Card Securely.
   - Confirm Clover-hosted fields appear and the POS retains only brand/last four.
