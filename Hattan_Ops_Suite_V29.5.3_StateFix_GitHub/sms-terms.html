Text message (SMS) program

What you'll receive: account and order notifications about your dry cleaning, laundry and alteration tickets at Hattan Cleaners — for example when your order is ready for pickup, a receipt when a card on file is charged, a notice if a card is declined, confirmation that a card was saved, and pickup reminders. We do not send marketing messages through this program.

How you sign up: at our counter, when you drop off an order or open an account, our staff asks whether you'd like order updates by text. If you say yes, staff records your consent in our point-of-sale system with your mobile number. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency varies with your orders (typically 1–4 messages per order). Message and data rates may apply.

To stop: reply STOP to any message. You'll receive one confirmation and no further texts. For help: reply HELP, call (212) 477-1740, or email hattancleaners141@gmail.com. To rejoin, reply START or ask us at the counter.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy policy

We collect the information you give us to serve your orders: your name, phone number, address (for delivery), email if provided, and your order history. Payment cards are saved and charged through our processor, Clover; we never see or store your full card number.

We use this information only to process your orders, send the notifications described above, and keep your account records.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Mobile numbers and text-messaging consent collected for this program are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. We share information only with service providers that help us run the business (for example our payment processor and our text-message provider, Twilio), solely to provide our services, or when required by law.

To see, update or delete your information, call (212) 477-1740 or email hattancleaners141@gmail.com.
