Text message (SMS) program

What you'll receive: service messages about your dry cleaning, laundry and alteration orders at Hattan Cleaners: drop-off confirmations and thank-you messages, order-ready alerts, pickup reminders, pickup thank-you messages, receipts with a link to view the receipt, notices when a saved card is charged or declined, card-saved confirmations, and store service notices such as changed hours, holiday closures and weather closures. We do not send marketing or promotional messages through this program.

How you sign up: texts are only sent to customers who say yes at our counter. Consent is optional and is not a condition of any purchase or service.

How customers opt in

When a customer drops off an order or opens an account at 141 3rd Avenue, the counter staff member reads this question from the point-of-sale screen:

📱 Ask the customer:

“Would you like order updates from Hattan Cleaners by text at this number? That includes when your order is ready, receipts, card charges and store closures. Message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP anytime to opt out.”

Customer said YESNo thanks

The box is never pre-selected. Only if the customer says yes does staff press “Customer said YES”. The system then saves the customer's mobile number, the date and time, and the staff member who recorded it. The customer immediately receives this confirmation text:

Hattan Cleaners: You're signed up for order updates by text. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

A sign at the counter reads: “Get order updates by text — just ask! Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Terms & privacy: hattan-ops-suite.netlify.app/sms-terms.html”.

Program details

Message frequency varies with your orders (typically 1–4 messages per order). Message and data rates may apply.

To stop: reply STOP to any message. You'll receive one confirmation and no further texts. For help: reply HELP, call (212) 477-1740, or email hattancleaners141@gmail.com. To rejoin, reply START or ask us at the counter.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy policy

We collect the information you give us to serve your orders: your name, phone number, address (for delivery), email if provided, and your order history. Payment cards are saved and charged through our processor, Clover; we never see or store your full card number.

We use this information only to process your orders, send the notifications described above, and keep your account records.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Mobile numbers and text-messaging consent collected for this program are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. We share information only with service providers that help us run the business (for example our payment processor and our text-message provider, Twilio), solely to provide our services, or when required by law.

To see, update or delete your information, call (212) 477-1740 or email hattancleaners141@gmail.com.
